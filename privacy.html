Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Client-Side Local Storage

Osiris Watch operates primarily as a client-side application. Your viewing preferences, watch history (playback progress and completed episodes), bookmarks, custom watchlist folders, theme choices, and preferred streaming server selections are stored locally in your browser's localStorage.

None of your personal watch history or list data is sold, monetized, or sent to tracking agencies. You can export, back up, or permanently wipe your local data at any time from the Settings page.

2. AI Search & Natural Language Queries

When you use the AI Discovery feature, your search query text and selected filtering parameters are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS to our AI processing endpoint powered by Groq. These queries are processed ephemerally solely to parse movie/show genres, mood, release eras, and relevant thematic titles.

AI search queries are never tied to any personal identity or stored in permanent personal user profiles.

3. Third-Party Media Embeds & TMDB Metadata

Osiris Watch does not host, store, or stream any media files on its servers. All movie and TV show titles, overviews, ratings, and backdrop artwork are sourced from The Movie Database (TMDB) API.

Video playback streams are rendered via sandboxed embedded iframes connecting directly to independent third-party hosting providers. When interacting with a third-party video player, that provider's independent privacy policy and terms apply.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For questions regarding our privacy practices or to provide feedback on data handling, join our community on Discord or view our DMCA Policy.
