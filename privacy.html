Privacy Policy

Osiris Watch is designed around minimal data collection and client-side control. You can browse catalog metadata, manage local watchlists, and watch embedded titles without submitting personal details or third-party ad tracking cookies to us.

1. Data Stored in Your Browser

Your preferences, watchlist items, custom folders, continue-watching history, recent searches, and display settings are saved locally in your browser via localStorage and IndexedDB. This data stays on your device unless you choose to export a backup or sign in to cloud sync.

  • Local control — Clear your watchlist, progress history, or export everything as JSON or CSV at any time from Settings.
  • No advertising profiles — Osiris Watch does not sell browsing history, build ad profiles, or run commercial ad-targeting pixels.

2. Catalog Requests & AI Search

Movie, series, and anime artwork and descriptions are requested through our catalog proxy and The Movie Database (TMDB). When you explicitly submit a query in AI Search mode, only your prompt and relevant title candidates are processed through our serverless search endpoint to rank matches.

3. Accounts & Watch Party Rooms

If you choose to create an account or join a Watch Party, authentication tokens, optional profile avatars, and temporary room sync state (chosen title, episode, provider, and room chat messages) are processed through our backend coordination API and Supabase only while you use those features. Temporary Watch Party rooms automatically expire after inactivity.

4. Third-Party Embed Providers & Ads

Video streams load directly inside third-party embed iframes hosted by external services (such as CineSrc, VidKing, VidSrc, and 111Movies). Those external hosts operate independently and enforce their own privacy rules, scripts, and pop-up advertisements outside our control.

For cleaner and safer playback, we strongly recommend enabling a trusted content blocker extension on desktop or an adblocker app on mobile (see our recommendations in Settings).

5. Contact & Legal

Questions about privacy practices or takedown requests can be shared via our Discord community, or review our DMCA & Copyright Policy.
